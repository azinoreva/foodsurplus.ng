How it Works

  1. Suppliers send in products that need to be consumed within a week — like fresh food, produce, dairy, or baked goods.
  2. Because the products have a short window, suppliers offer them at prices close to their own cost. This way they recover most of their money instead of taking a loss.
  3. Instant warehousing means we don't store anything long-term. Products move in and out within hours.
  4. Buyers order online. We pick, pack, and ship immediately — sometimes same day, always within 24 hours.
  5. Since nothing sits around, our storage costs are almost zero. We pass those savings directly to you.
  6. You get products at very low prices — much lower than retail or even wholesale — because you're buying at near-supplier cost with almost no warehousing markup.
